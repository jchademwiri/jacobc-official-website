## Bridging Operational Delivery with Financial Discipline

A project is only truly successful when it is delivered on time and fully paid. Many businesses struggle with the disconnect between on-site operations and financial accounts receivable, resulting in delayed billing, uncollected revenue, and cash-flow strain.

I provide operational project coordination integrated with strict enterprise financial governance using SAP ERP and Sage Accounting.

## Core Governance Pillars

- **Zero Revenue Leakage:** Ensuring every mobilized machine, completed civil milestone, or delivered service is invoiced accurately with proper client sign-offs.
- **Municipal & Corporate AR Resolution:** Navigating complex municipal payment workflows, purchase order amendments, and dispute resolution to recover outstanding receivables.
- **Audit-Ready Financial Records:** Maintaining complete audit trails linking contracts, delivery notes, tax invoices, and bank statements to satisfy internal and external auditors.
- **Agile Coordination:** Managing cross-functional project teams and suppliers with clear milestone tracking and accountable communication.

## Who This Benefits

- **Plant Hire & Logistics Enterprises:** Operating complex fleets where utilization rates, fuel allocations, and invoice accuracy directly dictate cash flow.
- **Municipal Service Contractors:** Managing multi-year government contracts requiring precise compliance reporting and structured invoicing.
- **Growing Companies:** Transitioning from manual bookkeeping to enterprise ERP systems like SAP and Sage.
