## Comprehensive Tender Management for Public & Private Contracts

Submitting tenders is not simply about filling out forms; it is about building a verifiable compliance system that withstands strict Supply Chain Management (SCM) scrutiny. From initial opportunity vetting to final tender box dispatch, I manage the complete tender lifecycle to maximize technical scores and ensure zero disqualifications.

## Why Bids Fail Before They Are Evaluated

The majority of tender disqualifications occur during the preliminary administrative compliance stage before technical or pricing points are ever scored:
- Missing mandatory statutory schedules or unsigned declarations of interest.
- Expired letters of good standing (COIDA) or tax clearance pins.
- Mismatched CIDB grading registrations or non-compliant joint venture agreements.
- Disorganized returnable volumes that fail to follow tender submission instructions.

My approach applies strict quality gating at every phase of bid compilation, ensuring complete compliance before the document is bound and submitted.

## Who This Service Is For

- **Civil Contractors & Plant Hire Operators:** Bidding on municipal infrastructure, roads, earthworks, and machinery hire tenders.
- **Logistics & Transport Businesses:** Pursuing fleet logistics, water tanker, and municipal waste management contracts.
- **Emerging & Growing SMEs:** Looking to navigate CIDB and CSD compliance to compete for larger municipal and corporate contracts.
