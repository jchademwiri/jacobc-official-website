Two weeks is enough to ship a small business site end to end, provided scope is locked before the first commit. The projects that slip are almost never slowed down by the build — they're slowed down by decisions still being made mid-sprint.

## 1. Freeze content before you freeze design

A design can't be finished against copy that's still changing. Get final copy and imagery signed off before the first layout is built.

## 2. Launch is a checklist, not a moment

Domain, analytics, redirects, forms and SEO basics should all be verified against a fixed checklist the day before launch, not discovered the day after.
