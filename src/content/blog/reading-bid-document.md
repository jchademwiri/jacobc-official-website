A bid document rewards a slow, literal first read. The clauses that disqualify submissions are rarely hidden — they're just easy to skim past on a first pass.

### 1. Separate mandatory from scored criteria

Mandatory requirements eliminate a bid outright; scored criteria only affect ranking. Triage the document into both lists before drafting a single response.

### 2. Annotate as you read, not after

Flag every date, certificate and format requirement inline the first time through. Reconstructing that list from memory later is where clauses get missed.
