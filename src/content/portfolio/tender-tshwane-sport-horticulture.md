Coordinated the Joint Venture (JV) bid submission for the City of Tshwane's horticultural maintenance of sport facilities (`ROC 07 – 2022/23`), spanning **seven municipal regions** and three distinct service sections. Awarded a **3-year as-and-when contract** effective February 2025.

## Joint Venture & Compliance Coordination

Joint venture bids carry heightened administrative risk because *both* partners must pass every compliance gate simultaneously:
- **JV Agreement & Combined Scoring:** Coordinated the formal Joint Venture agreement, consolidated B-BBEE certificate, and combined CSD/CIDB compliance verification across both entities.
- **Regional Resource Allocation:** Mapped equipment fleets (ride-on mowers, brushcutters, tractors, irrigation tools), supervisory teams, and regional depot logistics across all seven Tshwane regions.
- **SHEQ & Public Safety Files:** Built an **ISO 45001** and **ISO 14001** operational safety plan covering public-park operations, herbicide handling, and PPE compliance.

## Outcome

- **3-Year JV Contract Awarded:** Successfully won `ROC 07 – 2022/23` across seven regions, demonstrating the ability to align multi-company JV compliance under strict municipal evaluation rules.
