Prepared and submitted the winning bid for the City of Tshwane Human Settlements tender (`HHS 10 – 2023/24`) for the hire of **8 mobile drinking water tankers** (10,000–15,000 litre capacity) supplying potable water to informal settlements across the metro. Awarded a **3-year as-and-when contract** effective February 2025.

## Critical Compliance & Technical Requirements

Potable water supply tenders combine fleet compliance with strict public-health and food-grade tank standards:
- **Tank Hygiene & SANS 241 Readiness:** Compiled food-grade stainless/lined tank specifications, sanitization protocols, and water-quality testing procedures aligned with **ISO 9001** quality controls.
- **8-Truck Fleet Verification:** Assembled eNaTIS documentation, roadworthy certificates, driver PrDPs, and backup-tanker contingency plans for all 8 dedicated water tankers.
- **Mandatory SCM Gatekeeping:** Verified CSD, tax clearance, COIDA, and all MBD declarations with zero administrative defects.

## Commercial Modelling & Award

- **Route & Utilization Pricing:** Modelled per-kilometre, per-load, and daily standing rates across Tshwane's dispersed informal settlement zones so operations remain viable regardless of call-out distance.
- **Outcome:** Awarded a **3-year contract for all 8 trucks (`HHS 10 – 2023/24`)**, immediately transitioning into active daily dispatch and billing.
