A custom client self-service and billing portal engineered for Playhouse Media Group's clients, applying the same accounts receivable discipline I use in corporate ERP environments to digital client management.

## Architectural Highlights

- **Passwordless Authentication:** Built on Next.js 16 with **Better Auth**, supporting frictionless magic links and 6-digit verification codes so clients never struggle with forgotten passwords.
- **Invoice & Account Transparency:** Gives clients centralized access to quotes, active invoices, payment status, and project deliverables—reducing billing queries and speeding up collections.
- **Dedicated Admin Control Plane:** Backed by a separate administrative dashboard at `admin.playhousemedia.co.za` for account provisioning, invoicing, and status updates.
