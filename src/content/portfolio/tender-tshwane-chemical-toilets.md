Managed the full submission for this City of Tshwane Human Settlements tender (`HS 02 – 2025/26`), from initial RFP dissection and statutory compliance through to the commercial rate schedule for hire, servicing, delivery, and relocation of chemical toilets—including specialized accessible units for people living with disabilities. Awarded a **3-year as-and-when contract** effective 2026.

## Tender Scope & Administrative Gatekeepers

Municipal sanitation tenders carry strict administrative and environmental disqualifiers before pricing is even evaluated:
- **Mandatory Statutory Pack:** Verified active Central Supplier Database (CSD) registration, SARS tax compliance status, COIDA Letter of Good Standing, B-BBEE verification, and fully signed Municipal Bidding Documents (MBDs).
- **Environmental & Waste Disposal Compliance:** Demonstrated legal liquid-waste disposal agreements and municipal wastewater discharge permits aligned with **ISO 14001:2015** environmental controls.
- **Fleet & Unit Readiness:** Compiled proof of ownership, vehicle roadworthy certificates, and unit specifications covering standard and disability-accessible chemical toilets.

## Technical & Commercial Execution

- **Rate Schedule Structuring:** Priced unit hire, scheduled weekly/bi-weekly servicing, emergency call-outs, and site-to-site relocations across Tshwane's regions so margins remain sustainable across a 36-month term.
- **SHEQ & Operational Methodology:** Drafted a practical servicing, hygiene, and spill-containment methodology backed by our Integrated Management System (IMS) SOPs and HIRA risk assessments.

## Outcome

- **Contract Awarded:** Secured a **3-year municipal contract (`HS 02 – 2025/26`)** with zero administrative findings, transitioning directly into active contract delivery and SAP/Sage milestone billing.
