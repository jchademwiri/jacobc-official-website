Serving as appointed Management Representative leading the company-wide design, documentation, and implementation of an Integrated Management System (IMS) for Sithembe Transportation & Projects (STP) and affiliated Lifting Machinery Entity (LME) operations.

## The Challenge

Operating across corporate plant hire, municipal transport infrastructure, sanitation services, and heavy lifting machinery entities demands stringent multi-standard compliance. The organization required a unified operational framework satisfying three distinct ISO standards simultaneously, while aligning with statutory requirements under the Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Act and Driven Machinery Regulations (DMR 19 for lifting machinery).

## The Integrated Approach

Rather than maintaining fragmented departmental silos, I architected a unified High-Level Structure (Annex SL) integrating:
- **ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management):** Standardizing fleet maintenance workflows, client delivery tracking, subcontractor vetting, and contract invoicing accuracy via SAP and Sage.
- **ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management):** Formulating environmental aspect/impact registers, liquid waste handling procedures for sanitation contracts, and emergency spill containment protocols.
- **ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety):** Establishing comprehensive Hazard Identification and Risk Assessments (HIRA), operator accredited certification controls, statutory lifting equipment load testing schedules, and Incident Non-Conformance Reporting (NCR) workflows.

## Key Outcomes & Operational Governance

- **Complete Policy & Manual Suite:** Authored the overarching SHEQ Manual, mandatory system procedures, and task-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- **Statutory Audit Readiness:** Established structured internal audit schedules, legal compliance registers, and management review protocols preparing the entity for Stage 1 external certification.
- **Commercial Competitiveness:** Direct alignment of ISO compliance evidence into tender submissions, bolstering technical scores on high-value municipal and public-sector procurement bids.
- **Financial Integration:** Synchronized accounts receivable reconciliation with operational milestones in SAP and Sage, eliminating billing discrepancies and reducing payment cycle turnaround.
