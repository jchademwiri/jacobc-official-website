Managed the end-to-end bid submission for the City of Tshwane's Corporate Fleet Management vehicle hire tender (`SS 01 – 2023/24`), covering heavy commercial construction and refuse removal vehicles—including tipper trucks, water tankers, and rear-end loader (REL) waste compaction trucks. Awarded a **3-year as-and-when contract** effective May 2024 across **6 vehicle item categories**.

## Bid Preparation & Statutory Controls

- **Roadworthiness & Fleet Licensing:** Verified eNaTIS registration certificates, active operator cards, PrDP driver credentials, and comprehensive commercial insurance across all tendered vehicle classes.
- **Mandatory SCM Documentation:** Assembled complete MBD schedules, CSD tax compliance verification, B-BBEE documentation, and municipal rates clearance certificates with zero administrative omissions.
- **Operational SLA Commitments:** Structured response-time guarantees, backup vehicle replacement protocols, and preventative maintenance schedules aligned with **ISO 9001:2015** service quality standards.

## Commercial & Billing Structure

- **Transparent Rate Schedules:** Priced hourly, daily, and monthly wet-rate hire structures accounting for diesel utilization, refuse-compactor hydraulics maintenance, and municipal call-out patterns.
- **Audit-Ready Invoicing Design:** Aligned vehicle logbook formats with municipal Purchase Order (PO) requirements to prevent downstream payment disputes.

## Outcome

- **3-Year Contract Awarded:** Secured **6 vehicle item categories (`SS 01 – 2023/24`)**, transitioning seamlessly from bid award into active municipal fleet deployment and monthly AR reconciliation.
