**Apex Web Solutions** is the web development and managed hosting arm of Playhouse Media Group, packaging custom websites and e-commerce builds into transparent monthly subscription tiers (`R299–R999/mo`) for South African small and medium businesses.

## Engineering & Commercial Model

- **Productized Delivery:** Eliminates unpredictable agency retainers by offering clear scope, fast turnaround sprints, hosting, and ongoing maintenance under fixed monthly plans.
- **Modern Static Performance:** Built on **Astro.js** with clean semantic HTML, technical SEO schemas, and sub-second page loads that outperform bloated WordPress templates.
