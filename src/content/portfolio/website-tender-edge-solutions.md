**Tender Edge Solutions** is the tender-compliance consulting division of Playhouse Media Group, built to help Gauteng SMEs and emerging contractors eliminate administrative disqualifications on public and corporate bids.

## Core Focus & Positioning

- **Statutory Foundation:** Guides businesses through CSD registration, CIDB grading applications, COIDA Letters of Good Standing, SARS tax compliance, and B-BBEE affidavits.
- **End-to-End Bid Compilation:** Structures MBD/SBD returnable forms, compliance matrices, and technical methodology packs so smaller contractors can compete with institutional polish.
- **High-Speed Astro Architecture:** Engineered on **Astro.js** and Tailwind CSS for instant load times, clear service pathways, and direct consultation lead capture.
