Managing post-award execution and billing governance for the City of Tshwane Corporate Fleet Management construction equipment contract (`SS 02 – 2023/24`), covering primary and backup appointments across **40+ machine and equipment categories**.

## Mobilization, Safety & DMR 19 Governance

- **Multi-Category Call-Out Dispatch:** Coordinating rapid mobilization of excavators, graders, mobile cranes, compactors, and generators across municipal depots and roadworks sites.
- **ISO 45001 & DMR 19 Compliance:** Maintaining active load-test certificates, operator competency cards, and pre-mobilization safety checklists so equipment passes site inspections without downtime.

## Machine Utilization & Contract Billing

- **Daily Logsheet Reconciliation:** Auditing operator daily hours, standing time, and lowbed mobilization charges against municipal Purchase Orders in **SAP ERP and Sage Accounting**.
- **Dispute-Free Invoicing:** Ensuring every invoice carries signed municipal supervisor verification, eliminating payment rejections and protecting working capital.
