Coordinating ongoing fleet operations and accounts receivable for the City of Tshwane Corporate Fleet Management vehicle hire contract (`SS 01 – 2023/24`), managing **6 heavy commercial vehicle categories** including tipper trucks, water tankers, and rear-end loader (REL) refuse compaction trucks.

## Operational Execution & ISO 9001 Quality

- **Fleet Availability & Uptime:** Managing driver allocations, preventative maintenance windows, and rapid replacement units to meet strict municipal waste-collection and construction SLAs.
- **Safety & Roadworthiness Audits:** Tracking vehicle roadworthy renewals, PrDP expiries, and incident/non-conformance reporting (NCR) under our **ISO 9001 & ISO 45001** Integrated Management System.

## Financial Control & Invoicing

- **Trip & Tonnage Reconciliation:** Verifying daily vehicle logbooks, tip-tickets, and municipal call-out authorizations prior to monthly billing in **SAP ERP and Sage Accounting**.
- **AR Governance:** Monitoring municipal debtor ageing and resolving Purchase Order queries proactively to maintain consistent cash flow.
