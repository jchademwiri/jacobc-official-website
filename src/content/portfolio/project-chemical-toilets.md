Coordinating post-award operational delivery and contract billing on the City of Tshwane Human Settlements chemical toilet hire and servicing contract (`HS 02 – 2025/26`). Having led the winning tender submission, I oversee execution to ensure service-level targets, environmental compliance, and monthly invoicing stay synchronized.

## Operational Coordination & ISO Governance

- **Servicing & Relocation Scheduling:** Coordinating weekly vacuum-servicing routes, fresh-chemical replenishment, and rapid site-to-site unit relocations—including accessible units for persons with disabilities.
- **ISO 14001 & ISO 45001 Controls:** Enforcing spill-containment kits on service trucks, legal wastewater disposal manifests, and PPE/hygiene SOPs under our Integrated Management System.

## Contract Billing & Accounts Receivable (SAP & Sage)

- **Proof-of-Service Verification:** Reconciling signed municipal servicing sheets, delivery notes, and relocation orders against contractual Purchase Orders before issuing monthly tax invoices.
- **Zero Revenue Leakage:** Managing debtor follow-ups and municipal payment workflows in **SAP ERP and Sage Accounting** to keep cash collection predictable.
