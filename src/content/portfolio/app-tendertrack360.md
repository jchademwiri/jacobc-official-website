**TenderTrack360** is a specialized tender and contract-lifecycle SaaS platform I built directly from my day-to-day experience as a Tender Manager—designed to replace fragile spreadsheets with structured bid tracking, compliance alerts, and post-award contract visibility.

## The Problem It Solves

In active tendering environments, teams lose bids and revenue to avoidable gaps: missed briefing sessions, expired COIDA/tax certificates, and lost Purchase Order tracking after a contract is awarded.

## Core Platform Capabilities

- **Bid Pipeline & Deadline Alerts:** Tracks tenders from opportunity discovery and briefing attendance through compliance preparation, submission, and adjudication.
- **Compliance Document Expiry Tracking:** Automated reminders before CSD, CIDB, tax pins, or B-BBEE certificates lapse.
- **Post-Award Contract & PO Tracking:** Bridges the gap between winning a tender and managing active contract milestones, purchase orders, and billing status.
- **Role-Based Access & Admin Dashboard:** Multi-user team permissions backed by a dedicated admin control plane at `admin.tendertrack360.co.za`.
