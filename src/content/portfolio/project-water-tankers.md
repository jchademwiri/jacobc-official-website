Coordinating daily operations and contract billing for the City of Tshwane Human Settlements potable water supply contract (`HHS 10 – 2023/24`), deploying an **8-truck fleet** of 10,000–15,000 litre drinking water tankers to informal settlements.

## Daily Dispatch & Water Quality Governance

- **8-Truck Route Optimization:** Scheduling daily filling-point turnarounds and settlement delivery rounds across multiple Tshwane regions to meet community water demand without interruption.
- **ISO 9001 Quality & Hygiene Controls:** Enforcing scheduled tank sanitization logs, chlorine/SANS 241 water-quality checks, and vehicle safety inspections.

## Billing Accuracy & AR Collection

- **Load & Kilometre Verification:** Reconciling signed community/ward delivery slips, GPS route logs, and municipal Purchase Orders in **SAP ERP and Sage Accounting** to guarantee 100% auditable monthly billing.
