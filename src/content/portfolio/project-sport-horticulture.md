Managing post-award operational coordination and financial reconciliation for the 3-year Joint Venture horticultural maintenance contract (`ROC 07 – 2022/23`) across **seven regions** of the City of Tshwane.

## Regional Execution & JV Governance

- **Multi-Region Scheduling:** Coordinating mowing, turf maintenance, brushcutting, and facility upkeep schedules across seven municipal regions and three service sections.
- **SHEQ & Environmental Controls:** Enforcing **ISO 45001** equipment safety (PPE, brushcutter guards, site demarcation) and **ISO 14001** green-waste disposal protocols.

## JV Billing & Milestone Reconciliation

- **Joint Venture Cost & Revenue Tracking:** Reconciling regional completion certificates and municipal inspection sign-offs against JV invoicing schedules in **SAP ERP and Sage Accounting** for transparent partner reporting.
