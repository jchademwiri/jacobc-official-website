## Role & Operational Governance

At Sithembe Transportation & Projects (STP), I direct integrated operational governance across fleet logistics, earthmoving plant hire, and Lifting Machinery Entity (LME) operations. My responsibilities span statutory quality management, municipal tender acquisition, and corporate accounts receivable reconciliation.

## Integrated Management System (IMS) Leadership

As appointed Management Representative, I direct the development, rollout, and internal audit governance of the company's Integrated Management System uniting three ISO standards under the Annex SL high-level structure:

- **ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management):** Standardizing fleet maintenance workflows, plant hire mobilization, client service level agreements (SLAs), and billing verification.
- **ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management):** Formulating environmental aspects and impacts registers, spill response protocols, waste disposal tracking, and fleet carbon reduction initiatives.
- **ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety):** Designing baseline Hazard Identification and Risk Assessments (HIRA), standard operating procedures (SOPs), operator safety certifications, and lifting equipment load-test registers in compliance with the OHS Act and Driven Machinery Regulations (DMR 19).

## Municipal Tender Management & Procurement

- Lead end-to-end tender lifecycles: bid document analysis, returnable compliance scheduling, CIDB grading alignment, and submission.
- Managed successful municipal submissions for high-value tenders, including City of Tshwane tenders for plant hire equipment, commercial water tankers, and specialized mobile sanitation.
- Formulate compliant technical proposals incorporating statutory health & safety packs, equipment calibration certificates, and financial models.

## Enterprise Accounts Receivable & Financial Control

- Direct corporate accounts receivable (AR) using SAP ERP and Sage Accounting, ensuring billing integrity, credit control, and client account reconciliations.
- Lead debt recovery and resolve complex billing queries across municipal departments and commercial contractor accounts.
- Reconcile invoice milestones against operational delivery proof of service, eliminating revenue leakage and ensuring audit readiness.
