## Role Overview

At Babiya Geomatics and Trading, I coordinated the preparation, verification, and administrative compliance for public sector and corporate tender submissions across surveying and civil projects.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Tender Compliance Schedules:** Analyzed tender specifications to build compliance matrices covering returnable documents, mandatory statutory requirements, and technical prerequisites.
- **Statutory & Vendor Maintenance:** Managed Central Supplier Database (CSD) profiles, tax clearance certificates, B-BBEE documentation, and CIDB grading registrations to ensure continuous bidding eligibility.
- **Bid Compilation & Dispatch:** Assembled end-to-end tender packs, returnable schedules, and supporting pricing documentation, coordinating on-time physical and electronic bid delivery.
- **Office Administration:** Managed procurement administration, supplier communications, client invoicing records, and project documentation filing.
