## The Foundation of My Tendering Career

Babiya Geomatics and Trading is where I built the foundational discipline of my tendering career. Working across municipal, provincial, and private surveying and civil bids, this role instilled the meticulous attention to detail and zero-tolerance compliance standards required to compete and win in South African public procurement.

## Mastering Public Procurement Compliance

In public tendering, a single missing document, expired tax pin, or unsigned schedule triggers instant disqualification before technical pricing is ever evaluated. At Babiya, I learned to dissect bid documents from back to front:

- **Disqualification Criteria & Compliance Matrices:** Deconstructed complex Requests for Proposals (RFPs) and tenders to map every mandatory returnable document, functionality threshold, and evaluation phase.
- **Statutory Vendor Administration:** Managed and maintained active Central Supplier Database (CSD) profiles, SARS tax compliance status, CIDB grading alignments, B-BBEE affidavits, and COIDA Letters of Good Standing.
- **Returnable Schedule Preparation:** Compiled standard bidding documents (SBD forms), joint venture agreements, sub-contracting declarations, and commercial pricing schedules with total accuracy.

## Bid Assembly, Dispatch & Physical Submissions

- Assembled physical tender packs: indexing, page numbering, file separating, and binding multi-volume tender submissions.
- Managed time-sensitive dispatch routines, ensuring physical bid boxes across government departments and municipal tender offices were reached comfortably ahead of closing deadlines.
- Conducted post-submission administrative debriefs and maintained organized tender archive registers for statutory audit trails.

## A Bedrock for Future Operational Leadership

The hands-on ground work at Babiya established the core procurement instincts, compliance rigor, and administrative accuracy that later propelled my career at Sithembe Transportation & Projects: enabling me to lead multi-million Rand municipal tenders, build custom tender management software (TenderTrack360), and direct Integrated Management Systems under ISO standards.
